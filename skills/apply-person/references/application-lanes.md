# Application lane routing

Use the legal applicant and outcome to choose one lane. When an opportunity spans categories, choose the tab that best describes the outcome and cross-reference it in notes rather than creating duplicate rows.

## Jobs

Includes internships, employment, contract roles, and structured recruiting programs whose primary outcome is work. Inspect compensation, work mode, location, work authorization, start date, employment type, and interview stages.

## Degrees

Includes bachelor's, master's, PhD, professional, and other degree admissions. Inspect admission term, supervisor or department, funding, tuition, test requirements, transcripts, references, research proposal, and credential evaluation.

## Scholarships & Fellowships

Includes scholarships, fellowships, leadership programs, funded research placements, and individual awards whose main outcome is selection or funding. Inspect award amount, eligible costs, residency, attendance, deliverables, and whether a host nomination is required.

## Grants

Includes funding where the person is the applicant or named individual recipient. If a company, university, nonprofit, or other organization is the legal applicant, stop and explain that this personal-application system is not the correct ledger.

## Events & Workshops

Includes conferences, summits, workshops, summer/winter schools, travel programs, and research schools. Inspect event dates, attendance format, travel funding, fees, visa letters, poster/talk requirements, and schedule conflicts.

## Hackathons & Competitions

Includes hackathons, challenges, contests, and competitions. Inspect team rules, intellectual-property terms, submission artifacts, judging stages, prizes, travel, and time commitments.

## Custom lane

Use the user-created schema and status set from the private System Index. Preserve the final-review and claim-integrity rules regardless of lane.
